The artboard in two minutes

The board

  • Top bar: switch posts or start a New post, the post's ratio, References (a new carousel patterned after posts you like), Captions, Apply comments and Download all.
  • Post settings: series, subject, handle, the big text font, and your overall note for Claude.
  • Each slide card: the slide, your comment and status (Claude reads comments when you press Apply comments), and its settings: text, photos, stickers, layout, textures. Fold a card with its arrow, or all of them with Fold all slides (Ctrl+A).
  • Photo library (below the slides): upload photos and videos, search, folders. Select photos to move, Download or Put in frames. A video goes in any photo spot (Replace); videos over 20 MB are made smaller as they upload.

The large view (tap a slide)

  • Tap any text, sticker or photo to select it: drag to move, pull a handle to resize, the round one to turn. The bar under it: Edit, Font, Duplicate, Arrange, Position; a photo has Crop (with Turn and Straighten), Replace, Edit photo.
  • Toolbar: Sticker, Text, History (undo, redo, earlier versions), Texture (Text layers and Background layers you can stack and reorder), Frame, Edit photo.
  • Header: Preview (phone, profile grid, full screen), Download (PNG or JPG, the slide's size; Download video (MP4) on a video slide), ⋮ (search, duplicate in any size, size of this slide).
  • A video slide plays under the slide, with play, sound and a bar to scrub; the camera button makes the frame showing the slide's still.

Getting it out

  • Download all saves every slide (and transparent overlays for video slides). The PDF guide for ManyChat is on the post.
  • Everything saves as you go. Keyboard shortcuts: ⓘ → Keyboard shortcuts.

Keyboard shortcuts

Press Change, then the new keys (with Ctrl, ⌘ or Alt). A key that's already used swaps over. Saved in this browser.
